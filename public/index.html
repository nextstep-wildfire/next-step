Next Step

For California wildfire insurance claims

When your insurer says no, offers less, or goes quiet.

Next Step helps you choose one clear thing to ask for, and writes a short, polite letter you can send today. It also shows the usual steps of a claim, in order.

Start my letter

Free. No account. About 10 minutes.

General information, not legal or insurance advice. Next Step draws on public sources such as the California Department of Insurance. It can be wrong, and your own policy is what counts. Check every detail before you send anything.

How it works

  1. 1

    Say what happened

    Pick what your insurer did, then add a few lines in your own words.

  2. 2

    Choose one ask

    See every option with a plain note on when it fits. Pick the one that matters most right now.

  3. 3

    Copy your letter

    Edit it if you like, copy it into an email, and see what to do while you wait.

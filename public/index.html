After a fire, much of a claim happens in writing

A denial. An offer far below your contractor’s estimate. Then weeks of silence. Each one needs an answer, written down, while you handle everything else a fire leaves behind.

Next Step turns each of those moments into one clear request, in plain words, and tells you what happens next.

Every word to your insurer counts. Get each one right.

Ready for every exchange with your insurer

  • Know what to ask for

Questions before you start

Is Next Step free?

Yes. There is nothing to pay and no account to make.

Is a person reading what I write?

No. Next Step is an AI guide, not a person, and no one here will call you back. If you need a person, the public phone lines below are staffed.

Is this legal or insurance advice?

No. It gives general information drawn from public sources such as the California Department of Insurance and United Policyholders. It can be wrong, and your own policy is what counts.

What happens to what I type?

Your chats, letters and Record stay in this browser. When you ask for a letter or an answer, what you typed is sent to the AI to write it and is not stored by us. Leave out anything you do not want to share.

Will this get me more money?

It cannot promise any outcome. It helps you ask for one clear thing at a time, in writing, so your request is plain and on the record.

Does Next Step send the letter for me?

No. You copy it into an email to your adjuster, open it in your email app, or download it as a PDF. You decide when it goes.

What if my insurer writes back?

Paste their reply into the Desk. Next Step explains it in plain words, suggests what to ask for next, and writes your reply.

Can I use it in Spanish?

Yes. Everything meant for you is in Spanish. Letters to your insurer stay in English, which is what the adjuster reads, with a Spanish copy beside them.

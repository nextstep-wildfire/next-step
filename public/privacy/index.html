Privacy

Your chats, letters and Record stay in this browser. We do not keep them.

When you ask for a letter or an answer, what you typed is sent to the AI to write it and is not stored by us. When you paste a message from your insurer, it is sent to the AI to read and is not saved. Leave out anything you do not want to share, like account numbers.

We only count how many people visit, open the Desk, copy or email a letter, or download a PDF, with no cookies and nothing that says who you are.

En español: sus conversaciones, cartas e Historial se quedan en este navegador. Lo que escribe se envía a la IA para escribir la carta y no lo guardamos. Solo contamos visitas y acciones, sin cookies y sin nada que diga quién es usted.
